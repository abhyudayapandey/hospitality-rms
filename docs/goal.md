Project Goal
 
To build a lightweight, mobile-first Outlet Operations Management platform that brings inventory, orders, workforce rostering, events, and day-to-day HR operations into a single application, supported by secure, role-based access and AI-driven operational intelligence.
 
The platform should enable outlet teams to run daily operations from one place, while giving managers real-time visibility and proactive recommendations across functions.
 
A concise product statement would be:
 
> “One intelligent platform to manage stock, orders, people and operations across every outlet.”
 
 
 
The key design principles are simple for frontline users, configurable for different outlet structures, Workday-inspired contextual security, auditable transactions and workflows, and AI that predicts issues and recommends or initiates controlled actions rather than simply acting as a chatbot.
