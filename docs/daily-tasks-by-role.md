# Daily tasks by role

## What this is

This is the list of routine jobs we suggest for each role in your property. Today these jobs are done from memory or by being told. Once you confirm the list, we set them up in the app as repeating checklists and tasks, so they appear on each person's phone at the right time and nobody has to create them each day. Staff just tick them, enter a number (for example a fridge temperature) or take a photo.

Most tasks are **daily**. Weekly and monthly jobs are marked clearly in the "When" column.

## How to confirm

1. Read each role's table.
2. In the last column write **Keep**, **Change** (and what to change) or **Remove**.
3. Add any job we missed at the bottom of the role's table.
4. Send the marked-up list back to us. Department heads can adjust their own checklists later in the app.

## How to read the tables

- **How it's ticked**: _Tick_ means a simple done / not done. _Number_ means the person types a value (for example a temperature in °C, or cash in rupees); if the number is outside the safe range the manager is told. _Count_ means counting items and entering the quantity. _Photo_ means a photo is attached as proof.
- **Status**: _In the app today_ means this check is already set up as a checklist in the standard setup. _New – to confirm_ means we suggest adding it.
- **Who ticks**: the person in that role unless the table says otherwise.
- Times are suggestions for a typical day. We will change them to match your shifts.
- Roles that your property does not have, or departments you do not run (for example Banquets or Central Kitchen), can simply be marked Remove.

## Questions for the prospect

1. What are your shift times in each department (for example kitchen 06:00 to 14:00 and 14:00 to 23:00)? We will set task times from these.
2. How many services do you run a day (breakfast, lunch, dinner, late night, room service)? Do you want a set-up and close-down checklist for each?
3. Which outlets do you have: hotel restaurant, bar, standalone bar, banquet hall, guest house, central kitchen? Which of these roles do not exist at your property?
4. Bar and restaurant: do they share staff and a till, or are they run separately? Does the bar run its own stock and cash?
5. Who is the head of each department, and who should be told when a temperature or count is out of range?
6. Which temperature limits do you follow for chillers, freezers, hot holding and glass chillers?
7. Which cleaning jobs do you want done on a weekly or monthly list (deep clean, descale, pest control, fire equipment)?
8. How often do you count stock: daily rotating counts, weekly, monthly full count? Who counts?
9. Do you need photos for any job beyond the ones marked (for example room inspections, table set-up, closing the kitchen)?
10. How do you handle cash: who counts the float and the till, and when?
11. Is there anything particular to your property (a pool, spa, gym, rooftop, room service, laundry for outside guests) that needs its own checklist?
12. Are there any jobs on this list that you do not do, or do differently? Please mark them Change or Remove.
