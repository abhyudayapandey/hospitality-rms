# The UX audit plan

The audit (October 2026) walked the Passport Hotel demo as all 38 job roles at 380 px: 1,576
screens. Its report, with ten screens shown now and as proposed, is the "Outlet Ops UX audit"
page. The direction the owner approved: fewer words, a picture on everything, tap instead of
type, each person sees only their own work, and a photo always sits above the button that
finishes. Every PR below applies that to every role whose screens it touches, not only the ten
drawn, and puts screenshots of before and after in its description.
