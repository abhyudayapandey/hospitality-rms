# A restaurant with a bar: gaps from the dry run (ADR 139)

From the walk of [TEST] Restaurant on 13 October 2026, every role at 380 px.

1. **Push today shows food dishes to bar staff.** The bartender, bar back, steward and
   cashier see the same list. Filter by the menu the person's department sells from.
2. **Nobody can be given a repair.** The template has no technician. Let a repair go to a
   manager on shift or to an outside vendor (name and phone), closed with a photo.
3. **No starter SOPs** come with the restaurant and bar templates.
4. **The sous chef and assistant manager** work shifts but have no checklist of their own.
5. **The cashier only imports the till file.** Decide whether the cashier may type a day's
   sales when the till can't export.
6. **The GM approves every stock count** at an outlet with no cost controller. Fine for one
   outlet; a larger site may want an accountant to.
