Privacy

Draft for legal review. Based on what the app collects today.

What we collect

From this website: the name, phone number, email, business name and outlet details you give us to book a demo. We keep them so our team can call you, and delete them when you ask. From the app, for our customers' staff: name, phone number, the shifts you work, clock-in times with a live selfie and location, and the work you record.

How long we keep it

Clock-in selfies and locations are deleted on a fixed schedule. Bills and money records are kept for 7 years. Routine task photos are deleted after 30 days.

Where it is kept

In India, on Amazon Web Services in Mumbai.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal data, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
