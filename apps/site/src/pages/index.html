How Senior works

Senior salah deta hai. Faisla aapka.

Senior advises. You decide.

  • Senior notices what runs low, what is due and who hasn't come in.
  • Senior asks the right person, by role and by place. Never the whole group.
  • Nothing happens until a person says yes. Every order, roster change and approval is someone's tap, with their name on it.
For the people on the floor

Har naye ko, pehle din se ek Senior.

Every new person gets a Senior from day one.

  • Their own list, with a picture on every step and every item.
  • The house recipe, scaled to today's batch, with the method step by step.
  • No shouting across the kitchen to ask where the paneer is kept.

Senior helps your people. It doesn't replace any of them.
