    Before you start

    How long does set-up take?

    It depends on how many outlets you have. We load your outlets, people, items, suppliers and recipes from the spreadsheets you already keep, check them, and show you a dry run before anything goes live.

    Can I run many outlets or a franchise on Senior?

    Yes. One company can have many outlets, each with its own stores, people and checklists. An area manager sees every outlet they look after. Outlets share the same recipes, checklists and SOPs, and a central kitchen can send stock to them.
