How Senior works

Senior salah deta hai. Faisla aapka.

Senior advises. You decide.

  • Senior notices what runs low, what is due and who hasn't come in.
  • Senior asks the right person, by role and by place. Never the whole group.
  • Nothing happens until a person says yes. Every order, roster change and approval is someone's tap, with their name on it.

    Questions

    What owners ask before they start

    Is Senior a person?

    No. Senior is the app on your team's phones. We call it Senior because it does what your best senior does: it remembers, it reminds, and it asks before it acts.

    How long does set-up take?

    It depends on how many outlets you have. We load your outlets, people, items, suppliers and recipes from your spreadsheets, check them, and show you a dry run before anything goes live.

    Do my staff need email?

    No. Staff can sign in with their phone number, or a login ID printed on a sheet. Everyone sets their own password at first sign-in.

    Does it replace my POS?

    No. Senior works next to your POS. Your cashier imports each day's item sales, so stock used and sales line up.

    Where is my data kept?

    In India, on AWS in Mumbai. Each customer's data is kept apart in the database itself. See Trust.

    Can Senior look after more later?

    Yes. Tell us and we switch it on. Nothing you've recorded is lost when something is switched off.

    Trust

    Each person sees only their place and their work.

    Access is given to a job role at a place, and covers that place and everything under it. The database checks it on every request, so a mistake in a screen can't show the wrong data.

    Company └─ West region Area manager sees both hotels ├─ Seaside Hotel GM sees the whole hotel │ ├─ Kitchen Executive chef: kitchen and its store │ ├─ Bar Bar manager: bar, bar store, bar team │ └─ Housekeeping └─ City Hotel

    Senior only advises

    Senior can read and suggest. A person approves every change, and their name is on it.

    Your data stays yours

    Every customer's data is kept apart inside the database, and every table is checked by an automatic test on every release.

    Nothing changed quietly

    Every stock movement and approval is recorded with who, when and why. Stock is never edited, only added to or taken from.

    Nobody approves their own request

    Requests go to the right person by place and role. Whoever asks can never be the one who approves.

    Kept in India

    On AWS in Mumbai. Bills are kept for 7 years. Clock-in selfies and locations are deleted on a fixed schedule.

    Sign-in that suits a kitchen

    Staff sign in with their phone number. Clock-in takes a live selfie, on phones you've approved.

    What we collect

    From this website: the name, phone number, email, business name and outlet details you give us to book a demo. From the app, for our customers' staff: name, phone number, the shifts you work, clock-in times with a live selfie and location, and the work you record.
